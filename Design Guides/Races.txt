RACE: Coloquial Name / Formal Name

1. Who were they before the Fall of the Legacy?
2. What role did they play in the ancient world?
3. What fragment of that civilization do they still preserve?
4. What does the average member of this race look like? (silhouette, posture, clothing, colors)
5. How do they build, craft, or otherwise solve practical problems?
6. What is their relationship with technology and the Legacy?
7. How do they view magic? Is there even a distinction?
8. What virtues does their culture admire? What behaviors are looked down upon?
9. What occupations, skills, and talents are they naturally drawn toward?
10. What does one of their settlements feel like? (architecture, lighting, sounds, smells)
11. What does their art, music, and decoration emphasize?
12. What foods do they grow, gather, or prepare?
13. How do they name individuals and places? What does their language sound like?
14. Why would one of them leave home to become an adventurer?
15. How do the other races misunderstand them?
16. What mystery about their past is still unsolved?
17. What visual motif should immediately identify their civilization or ruins?
18. If this race vanished tomorrow, what unique piece of the world would be lost forever?
